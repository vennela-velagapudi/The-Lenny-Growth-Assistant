## Framework Principles
*   **Digital Writer Mindset**: Shift from consuming to creating. Don't wait for inspiration; build a daily writing habit (publish 30 short essays in 30 days). Write in public to get immediate data and feedback.
*   **The Endless Idea Generator**: Generate ideas by intersecting your interests with problems people have. Brainstorm specific answers to specific questions rather than general thoughts.
*   **The 4A Paths**: Structure writing around four paths:
    1.  **Actionable**: How-to guides and tutorials.
    2.  **Analytical**: Breakdowns, frameworks, and deep dives.
    3.  **Aspirational**: Stories of growth, lessons learned, and motivation.
    4.  **Anthropological**: Observations on human nature, trends, and society.
*   **Headline Frameworks**: The headline is 80% of the work. Use proven templates that promise a specific benefit to a specific audience. Clear > Clever.
*   **Skimmability**: Use short paragraphs (1-3 sentences max). Employ bolding, bullet points, and headers. White space is your friend. Treat the essay like a highly structured outline.
*   **Writing Rhythms**: Maintain momentum by separating the writing process into distinct stages: ideating, outlining, drafting, and editing. Do not edit while drafting.
*   **Content Differentiation**: Stand out by picking a specific niche, sharing unique personal stories, or combining two unrelated topics to create a new category.
*   **Twitter Thread Structure**: Adapt the essay format for social media. Start with a hook tweet, follow with a sequence of high-value points (one idea per tweet), and end with a summary/call to action.
*   **Naming/Claiming a Category**: Don't compete in an existing crowded market. Coining a new term for your specific approach establishes you as the default expert in that micro-category.
